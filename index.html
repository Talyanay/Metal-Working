Who We Are

Experience You Can Trust

At A. Yanai Industries Ltd. (תעשיות א.ינאי בע"מ), we bring over 40 years of expertise to the metal field. We pride ourselves on delivering high-quality solutions for both industrial and private clients.

We specialize in working with a wide variety of materials including Steel, Aluminium, Brass, and Stainless-steel. Our commitment to precision and durability has made us a leader in the local industry.

Our Capabilities

Bending

Precision CNC bending for complex shapes and angles across all metal types.

Rolling

Sheet metal rolling services for cylinders, cones, and curved components.

Welding

Professional welding (MIG/TIG) ensuring strong, clean, and durable joints.

Cutting

Laser and plasma cutting for high-accuracy parts and intricate designs.
