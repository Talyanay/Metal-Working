Our Capabilities

Advanced metalworking solutions for every industry.

CNC Bending

We utilize state-of-the-art CNC Press Brakes to deliver precise bending services. Our machines can handle complex geometries and ensure high repeatability for large production runs.

  • Complex shapes capabilities
  • Up to 6 meters length
  • Heavy duty plates bending

Sheet Metal Rolling

Our rolling department is equipped to roll sheets into cylinders, cones, and arcs. We specialize in rolling various thicknesses, ensuring the material structural integrity remains intact.

  • Cylinders & Cones production
  • Various diameters available
  • Perfect for tanks and pipes
  • Steel & Stainless Steel rolling

Professional Welding

Our certified welders are experts in MIG and TIG welding techniques. We provide strong, clean, and aesthetic welds suitable for both heavy industrial machinery and fine display products.

  • Certified professional welders

  • Assembly & Finishing
