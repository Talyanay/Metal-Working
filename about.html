Our Story

More than 40 years of excellence in metalworking.

Family Business, Industrial Power

Founded in 1980 by Mr. Yanai, our company started as a small workshop with a single vision: to provide uncompromising quality in metal solutions.

Over the decades, we have grown into a leading factory in the Israeli industrial zone, but we never lost our family values. We treat every client—whether a large factory or a private customer—as part of our family.

Today, A. Yanai Industries combines traditional craftsmanship with modern CNC technologies to deliver precise Bending, Rolling, and Welding services.

Our Journey
